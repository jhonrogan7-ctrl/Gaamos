Menu-build wizard — Gate 1: structure & prices

Three layouts for the same screen. Cards throughout, no tables. Every card edits in place, deletes, and moves; sections rename, reorder and take an icon.

The gate: a section must be ticked prices confirmed against the photo before the build advances — sections, not rows, so a 159-item card is ~10 ticks. This exists because the extractor never emits a null price: it invented one for all 27 prices it could not read, so blocking on missing prices would block on nothing.

📱 Mobile — 360 px

Two treatments of the same gate. Both are real layouts, not the desktop grid squeezed.

What changes on touch, and why. The desktop cards reveal edit and delete on hover — and hover does not exist on a phone. So on mobile every card carries a visible ⋯, the price is tappable on its own (the one field actually being checked), and the photo becomes a peek bar rather than a pane, because a side-by-side split at 360 px gives neither half enough room.

Mobile decisions worth your opinion

  • The price is its own tap target. It is the one field gate 1 exists to check, so it is a button, not text — tap it, a number pad opens, done. Everything else is behind ⋯.
  • One card per row. Two would fit at 360 px but the name would truncate, and a truncated dish name is exactly what you cannot verify against a photo.
  • M1 keeps the photo out of the way until you ask; M2 keeps it on screen and pays for it with vertical room.
  • M2 is the better phone design and the worse desktop one — so mobile and desktop need not be the same variant. Picking B for desktop and M2 for phone is a legitimate answer.

What every card does, in all three

  • Edit in place — name, price, description, dietary tags. No modal, no separate page.
  • Delete — the row is scratch space; nothing has touched the tenant yet.
  • Split into variants — 200/260 becomes two cards sharing a base name.
  • Move to another section — drag, or pick from the card's menu.
  • Add a row — the dashed card at the end of every section.
  • Printed text kept — where the read name differs from the card, the original shows under it, so a correction is never guesswork.
  • Flagged cards earn the warm border: split from a shared line, low confidence, or a price the model could not actually read.

Sections

  • Rename, reorder, change the suggested icon, or confirm prices — the icon comes from category_icons.for_section() and the venue's own wording is never rewritten.
